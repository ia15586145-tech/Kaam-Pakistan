# Privacy Policy for Kaam Pakistan

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2026

Kaam Pakistan ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Kaam Pakistan mobile application (package name: `com.aistudio.kaampakistan.mxqlv`). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account & Profile Information:** Name, phone number (for OTP verification), city/area, and service trade details (for workers).
- **Usage & Device Data:** Basic app performance and device diagnostics to improve app stability.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To verify user accounts via phone OTP.
- To connect customers with local service providers and skilled workers in Pakistan.
- To maintain safety, prevent spam, and provide customer support.

## 3. Data Sharing & Security
We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Contact details of registered workers are displayed within the app only so customers can hire them for services. We use secure authentication and database protection to keep your data safe.

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion
You can update your profile or request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us.
